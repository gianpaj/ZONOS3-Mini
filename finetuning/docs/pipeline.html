delayed audio rows: frame f of codebook j in row f + j, then one EOA per codebook
  • Grey rows are context only. Orange rows carry the loss.
  • The logits of a row predict the audio columns of the next row.
  • Ignored: the leading delay triangle and the padding after each EOA.
  • One sequence per forward, fresh state, so nothing leaks between examples.
